Conventions. k∥ = 0.5124·√(hν−Φ)·sinα,   kz = 0.5124·√((hν−Φ)cos²α + V₀) (free-electron final state), with work function Φ = 4.2 eV; binding energy at EF. In-plane reciprocal: square 2π/a (Γ–X boundary π/a, Γ–M boundary √2·π/a); hexagonal (Γ–M boundary 2π/√3a, Γ–K boundary 4π/3a). Out-of-plane 2π/c (Γ, Z at half). A mode uses a tilt-scan: slit angle α ∈ θcenter ± θmax along the cut, tilt β ∈ [βmin, βmax] about the cut axis, giving kx = kf·sinα, ky = kf·cosα·sinβ. DA mode shows the deflector disk.
